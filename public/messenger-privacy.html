Messenger privacy notice

Updated November 3, 2026. This notice applies to the BarkAi assistant for the Prime Fusion Cars Facebook Page and the Meta application “ИИ-ассистент Prime Fusion” (1627739205746711). Prime Fusion Inc operates this service to answer rental questions and help visitors understand the application process.

Information used

When you message the Page, Meta sends the service your message and technical event information. The application stores message text, the Page-scoped sender identifier, message identifiers, timestamps, the language you choose, and processing and sending status. It uses a limited recent conversation history to keep answers relevant. The current Messenger assistant handles text questions and language buttons. Enter rental documents and application details in the designated application on the Prime Fusion website.

Purpose and service providers

We use Messenger information to answer your questions, maintain conversation continuity, respect requests to stop, prevent duplicate replies and reconcile delivery errors. Automated answers can be inaccurate. Confirm individual rental terms and availability with the company.

Railway hosts the application and its conversation storage. Moonshot/Kimi processes questions and limited recent conversation context to generate or translate answers. OpenAI processes a question or its translated form to create an embedding for knowledge search. Supabase provides the reference knowledge and vector search used to find relevant company information. These providers receive the data needed for their part of the service. Processing may take place outside your location. Meta also handles messages under its own policies. We do not sell Messenger conversation data.

Retention and controls

The application removes routine message records older than seven days. Session data, stop or handoff flags, and records with uncertain delivery outcomes may be retained longer for access controls, respecting your communication choice and manual reconciliation. Provider logs or backups can follow the applicable provider's retention practices. We use HTTPS, access controls and Meta webhook signature verification.

Your requests

You may stop automated replies by sending STOP in the conversation. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact info@primefusioncars.com. Identify the Prime Fusion Cars Page and the approximate date of your conversation. We may ask you to confirm the request from the same Messenger account to protect another person's records. Follow the Messenger data deletion instructions. We review requests for records under our control and explain any necessary retention or provider limitations.
